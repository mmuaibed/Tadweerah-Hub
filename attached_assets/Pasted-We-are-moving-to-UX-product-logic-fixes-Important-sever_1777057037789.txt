## Priority 1 — Prevent bidding against yourself

Issue:
A buyer can increase their own bid without warning or restriction.

Required:

* If the company is already the highest bidder:

  * do NOT allow silent self-increase
  * show clear warning/action handling
* API must protect this, not only frontend.

Expected:

* A company cannot unknowingly bid against itself.
* If there is a valid business reason to allow it, it must require explicit confirmation and be clearly explained.

Please propose the safest approach before implementing if there is any ambiguity.

---

## Priority 2 — Auction pricing UX

Issue:
Buyer enters price per unit, but users think in total amount.

Example:
“I want to buy 9 tons for 1,000 SAR — what do I enter?”

Required:

* Allow buyer to enter either:

  * total price
  * or price per unit
* Auto-calculate the other value.
* Display both clearly before submit.

Expected:

* Buyer understands exactly what they are offering:

  * quantity
  * unit price
  * total price

---

## Priority 3 — Auction card clarity

Issue:
Marketplace/participation cards do not show enough auction context.

Required:

* Show highest current offer on the card
* Show my offer amount if I participated
* If my offer is not highest, show:

  * “عرضك ليس الأعلى”
  * highest offer amount
* If my offer is highest, show:

  * “أنت أعلى عرض”

Expected:
User should not need to enter listing details to understand auction position.

## Priority 6 — Marketplace separation

Issue:
Market shows my own listings mixed with others, which may confuse users.

Please propose the best UX:
Option A:

* Marketplace only shows other companies’ listings
* My Listings remains separate

Option B:

* Marketplace includes all listings but clearly marks “إعلاني”

Our preference:

* Marketplace should focus on opportunities from others
* My Listings should manage my own listings

Please confirm and implement the cleaner approach.
