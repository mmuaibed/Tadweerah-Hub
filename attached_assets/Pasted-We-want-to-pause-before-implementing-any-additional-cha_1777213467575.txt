## 1) Strategic Direction

We are aligned that:

Tadweerah is currently a simple operational MVP marketplace that supports:

onboarding → listings → marketplace → offers → deal → timeline → report → dashboard

We are NOT:

* rebuilding the product
* introducing complex flows
* turning this into a regulatory or compliance platform

Long-term, Tadweerah may evolve into a traceability and data layer supporting the circular economy.

But for now:
→ the goal is a simple, reliable system that supports real transactions.

---

## 2) What Must Remain Stable

We want to preserve:

* listing creation flow

* deal lifecycle and timeline
* dashboard and notifications

No rebuild.
No major new flows.
No compliance logic.
No unnecessary schema-heavy changes.

## 4) Key Gaps Identified (Critical)

Based on our combined review:

### A) Contact visibility in deal flow

Contact data already exists in the API, but is not visible in the UI.

This creates a critical operational gap:
after a deal is accepted, parties cannot easily contact each other.

→ This is a priority issue affecting real-world usability.

---

### B) Material category visibility

Categories exist in the system but are not visible in:

* listing cards
* listing detail
* deal panel
* reports

→ This affects decision-making for buyers.

---

### C) Deal reference (customer-facing)

We do NOT want a new ID system.

We want a formatted reference derived from existing data, such as:

TDW-2026-A3F2C1

→ For communication, tracking, and professionalism.

---

## 5) Lightweight Enhancements Under Consideration

We are only considering small, high-impact improvements:

* visual progress bar for deal timeline (UI only)
