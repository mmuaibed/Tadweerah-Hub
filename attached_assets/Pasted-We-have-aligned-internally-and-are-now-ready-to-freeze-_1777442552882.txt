We have aligned internally and are now ready to freeze the Tadweerah Operating Model as the official project charter before creating the final draw.io diagrams.

This is the complete and final version of the workflows and system behavior.

---

1. Marketplace Core Behavior

- Accept offer = accept one offer + reject all others + close listing + create deal (atomic).
- Listing is always closed after acceptance.
- No reopening of listings.
- If the deal fails or expires, a new listing must be created.

---

2. Accepting a Lower Offer (Simplified Governance)

- Producer cannot accept an offer that is not the highest active offer.
- If the producer wants to accept a lower offer:
  - They must first reject all higher offers.
  - Once the desired offer becomes the highest, they can accept it.
- No accepted_below_highest flag or schema changes.

---

3. Offer Behavior

- Buyers can resubmit after withdrawing.
- The new offer must exceed the current highest valid offer.
- Self-bidding is blocked.
- No role-based blocking using company_type.

---

4. Pricing Models

- Marketplace supports:
  - Fixed
  - By weight
- revenue_share is removed completely from Marketplace.
- It may be considered later in Contract Track if needed.

---

5. Deal Lifecycle (with Flexibility)

Deal can end in:
- completed
- expired
- cancelled

Pre-expiry policy:

Before a deal reaches expiry, the system notifies the producer.

Producer can:
1. Close immediately → status = cancelled
2. Extend once → for a limited number of days

Rules:
- Extension allowed once only
- After extension expires → deal becomes expired automatically
- No second extension
- No extension after dispatch
- If no action is taken → deal expires as per default rules

---

6. Required Workflows

Add before finalization:

WF-00: Pilot User Onboarding

- No open self-registration during pilot
- Companies verified before activation

CWF-08: Contract Completion
- Seller completes contract
- Only allowed when all shipments are closed/resolved
- Contract becomes terminal (completed)
- Both parties notified

---

7. Contract Track Principles

- Contract Track is a separate parallel system.
- Used for known parties and repeated transactions.
- Marketplace = discovery
- Contracts = execution

Strict isolation:
- Shared only: users and companies
- No listing/offer/deal references inside contracts
- No contract references inside marketplace
