We want your neutral assessment before deciding whether to implement a public / pilot landing page.

This is NOT an implementation request.

Please review the concept as CTO + Product Manager and give us an honest recommendation.

---

## Context

The platform is now technically ready for pilot.

Currently, users can access the app and proceed through the operational workflow, but we are considering whether the first public-facing page should do more to build trust, reduce resistance to change, and explain the pilot clearly.

The concern is that some users may enter the platform without a prior conversation with us, or may need reassurance before registering.

---

## Proposed Landing Page Concept

The landing page would be designed not only as a marketing page, but as a trust-building page for the pilot phase.

Its goals would be:

1. Help visitors understand Tadweerah within seconds
2. Reassure them that this is a limited, safe pilot — not a long-term commitment
3. Reduce resistance to adopting a new workflow
4. Clarify that Tadweerah does not replace existing relationships, but organizes and supports them
5. Encourage interested companies to join the pilot

---

## Proposed Sections

### 1) Hero Section

Explain clearly what Tadweerah does:

A B2B platform that helps companies list recyclable waste or surplus materials, connect with relevant buyers/processors/service providers, track transactions, and document outcomes.

Primary actions:

* Join the pilot
* Request a demo
* Login

Trust line:
Limited pilot — no long-term commitment — direct support from Tadweerah.

---

### 2) Trust Before Features

Explain that the pilot is designed as a controlled experiment, not a full operational change.

Potential messages:

* No need to change current workflows immediately
* Start with one use case
* Control what you want to test
* Tadweerah team supports the process
* The customer decides based on results

---

### 3) What Tadweerah Solves

Explain the operational problems:

* Manual coordination
* Unclear offers
* Difficult comparison
* Weak transaction documentation
* Missed value from surplus/waste materials
* Lack of structured visibility

---

### 4) How Tadweerah Works

A simple process:

1. Add available material or waste
2. Define visibility / target audience
3. Receive offers or interest
4. Select the best offer
5. Track deal progress
6. Get a transaction record/report

---

### 5) Control & Privacy

Clarify that companies stay in control:

* They control what they list
* They control who can see it
* Sensitive data is not exposed unnecessarily
* The platform supports documentation and traceability

---

### 6) Value by Segment

Explain value for:

* Waste generators / factories / companies
* Collectors / transporters
* Recyclers / processors
* Operational partners

---

### 7) Pilot Program

Explain the pilot:

* Limited number of companies
* Short onboarding
* One controlled use case
* Feedback-driven improvement
* No commercial commitment during the pilot unless agreed separately

---

### 8) CTA / Interest Form

Potential form fields:

* Company name
* Contact person
* Phone
* Email
* City
* Organization type
* Short description of need
