* create auction listing
* submit offer from another company

* listing requires capability/service
* company without capability is blocked
* company with capability but unapproved license is blocked if service requires license
* approved company with capability is allowed

## 5) Direct sale targeting

* open direct sale visible to all
* category-targeted visible only to matching category
* specific-company visible only to target company
* non-targeted company cannot see or submit

## 6) Revenue sharing

* appears only for Direct Sale
* percentage required when selected
* rejected for Auction
* visible in card, details, and deal panel

## 7) Deal flow

* accept offer
* confirm payment
* dispatch
* receipt
* confirm stage labels, timeline, and turn indicators update correctly

## 8) Value layer

* deal value summary works
* governance timeline works

## 9) Members

* owner can invite/remove

## 10) Trust / UX issues

* no “email not verified” warning appears in demo flow unless necessary
* no confusing system messages
