Additional future scenario for architectural alignment

We want to raise one important future use case:

A producer (for example, a recycling company) may want to create a listing targeted to:
- one specific buyer
- or a limited set of buyers

instead of publishing it to the full public marketplace.

In that scenario:
- the listing is not public
- only the intended buyer(s) can view it
- only the intended buyer(s) can submit offers
- the same pricing and offer logic still applies

-----------------------------------
Why this matters

This is not just a nice-to-have.

It matters because some companies may use the platform not only as an open marketplace, but also as an operating system to manage transactions with existing clients under long-term commercial relationships.

This can create major value for the platform because it supports:
- enterprise adoption
- operational execution inside the same system
- stronger compliance and traceability for regulators
- and a possible additional revenue stream for the platform

So we want to make sure the architecture does not unintentionally assume that all listings are always public.

-----------------------------------
Current direction we are considering

We agree that adding a passive structural field such as:

visibility = public | private

at the listing level appears to be a reasonable direction.

From our side, we are comfortable with this idea if:
- it is the correct long-term place for this concept
- it remains a passive structural addition only
- it creates no current UI or flow changes
- and it helps us avoid future architectural rework

But we do NOT want to treat this as a fixed instruction without your final judgment.
