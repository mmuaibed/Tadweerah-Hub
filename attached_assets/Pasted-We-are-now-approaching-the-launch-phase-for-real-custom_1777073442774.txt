We are now approaching the launch phase for real customers.

At this stage, the platform is technically solid. Our focus is shifting from building core functionality to increasing perceived value, trust, and conversion.

We want to add a small set of **high-impact, low-effort enhancements** that can significantly improve how customers perceive the platform and their willingness to adopt and pay for it.

Below are a few initial ideas (not final decisions):

---

## Suggested Value Enhancements (for your evaluation)

1. Deal Value Insight

* Show value gained (difference between first and highest offer)
* Optionally show percentage improvement

2. Stronger Dashboard Summary

* Listings, offers, active deals, completed deals
* Total quantity and total value

3. Smart Value Messages

* Context-aware messages explaining benefits:

  * competition improves pricing
  * transactions are documented
  * completed deals are tracked in performance

4. Compliance-Oriented Wording

* Improve report language to emphasize traceability and internal governance
* Avoid any wording that implies official certification

5. Light “Impact Summary”

* Total volume processed
* Number of deals
* Estimated operational value
* With clear disclaimer (operational/estimated only)

6. Partner Positioning Message

* Small message encouraging early adopters:
  “Early partners help shape the platform and gain priority in upcoming features.”
