## 1) Product Direction

Our current direction remains unchanged:

Tadweerah is now a simple operational MVP marketplace that supports:

* company onboarding
* listing creation
* marketplace browsing
* offers / bidding
* direct sale targeting
* deal lifecycle
* timeline
* reporting
* dashboard
* notifications

We do NOT want to rebuild the product or turn it now into a complex regulatory/compliance platform.

Long term, Tadweerah may evolve into a traceability and data platform supporting the circular economy, but the current goal is:

→ launch a practical MVP that can support real transactions with real customers.

### C) Simple pickup location details

### D) Visual deal timeline

No new states.

Not a full admin system yet.
