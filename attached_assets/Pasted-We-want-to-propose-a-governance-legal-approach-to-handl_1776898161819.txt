We want to propose a governance + legal approach to handle the “accepting a lower offer” scenario, and get your assessment before any implementation.

Objective:
Allow producers the flexibility to choose a non-highest offer when justified, while protecting the platform from legal exposure and maintaining transparency.

Proposed approach (policy + system support):

1) Terms & Conditions (T&C)
- Explicitly state that the producer is not obligated to accept the highest price.
- The producer has the right to choose any offer based on commercial, operational, or compliance considerations (e.g., logistics capability, buyer reliability, contract alignment).
- The decision is the responsibility of the producer as the owner of the goods.
- The platform is not a party to the commercial decision and bears no liability for the producer’s selection.

2) Internal accountability (producer side)
- The decision to accept a lower offer must be made by an authorized user within the producer organization.
- Any consequences of selecting a non-highest offer are treated as an internal decision within that organization.

3) Platform-level transparency & audit (minimal system support)
- All offers (including rejected ones) remain recorded.
- Rejection reasons are stored per offer.
- The accepted offer is clearly recorded.
- In internal reporting (for the producer), highlight when the accepted offer is not the highest (e.g., “accepted below highest” flag).
- The platform maintains an audit trail sufficient for review if needed.

4) No immediate change to core flow (MVP)
- No enforcement change required now.
- No change to current acceptance logic for MVP.
- This is a governance layer + light reporting/flagging.

Questions for your architectural/product judgment:

1) Do you consider this approach sufficient from a system + governance perspective for MVP?
2) Do you see any gaps that could still expose the platform to risk (technical or legal) despite the T&C + audit trail?
3) From a system design perspective, what is the minimal additional support you would recommend now (if any), without expanding scope?
   Examples:
   - a flag when accepted offer < highest historical
   - linking acceptance decision with existing rejection reasons
4) Would you recommend keeping this purely as policy for now, or adding a lightweight system signal (without enforcing new flows)?

Constraints:
- No expansion of current M4.5 scope
- No complex enforcement now
- UAT proceeds as planned

Goal:
Balance producer flexibility, platform protection, and auditability,
while avoiding premature complexity.
