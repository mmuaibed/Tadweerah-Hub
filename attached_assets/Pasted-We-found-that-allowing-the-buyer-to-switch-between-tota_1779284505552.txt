We found that allowing the buyer to switch between total amount and unit price during bidding creates rounding issues and business confusion.

New business rule:
Each listing must have one pricing method only, defined by the listing itself:
1) total_price listing
2) unit_price listing

The bidder should not choose between total and unit during bidding.

Required behavior:

A) Listing pricing method
Inspect the current listing schema and creation flow.
If a pricing method field already exists, use it.
If not, propose the safest additive field before implementation.

Allowed pricing methods:
- total_price
- unit_price

B) Buyer bidding screen
In listing-detail.tsx, remove the toggle that allows switching between "unit price" and "total amount" during bidding.

If listing pricing method = total_price:
- Show only one input:
  "Total offer amount before VAT"
- Use this value directly as the offer subtotal.
- Calculate VAT 15% from this subtotal.
- Show total including VAT.
- Do not calculate or display unit price.

If listing pricing method = unit_price:
- Show only one input:
  "Unit price before VAT"
- Calculate subtotal = unit price × listing quantity.
- Calculate VAT 15% from subtotal.
- Show total including VAT.
- Do not allow entering total amount manually.

C) Seller offer review
Show the offer according to the listing pricing method:
- For total_price listings: show submitted total amount before VAT, VAT, total including VAT.
- For unit_price listings: show unit price, quantity, subtotal before VAT, VAT, total including VAT.

D) Deal creation
Preserve the same pricing method from listing to offer to deal.
For total_price listings:
- Deal subtotal should be the submitted total offer amount.
For unit_price listings:
- Deal subtotal should be unit price × quantity.

E) Rounding/formatting
Format SAR amounts consistently:
- Maximum 2 decimals.
- No 10,000.008 style values.
- VAT should be calculated from the correct subtotal source.

F) Constraints
- Do not implement multi-material bundles.
- Do not change deal lifecycle states.
- Do not change atomic accept logic except to use the correct subtotal source.
- Do not refactor unrelated files.
- Do not touch admin, MWAN, transport responsibility, or Smart-Assist Transport.
- Keep this patch focused on pricing method and bidding clarity only.

Before implementation, confirm:
1) Does a listing pricing method already exist?
2) Which files will change?
3) Is a DB migration required?
4) How old listings will be handled safely?
5) How offer comparison will work for total_price vs unit_price listings.
