## 11) Eligibility & Licensing (Controlled Participation)

During company registration or profile setup:

Collect:

* License number
* License document (image)

Add status field:

* Pending (under review)
* Approved (eligible)
* Rejected (not eligible)
* Expired (license expired)

Behavior:

* All users can view the marketplace

* Only Approved users can:

  * Submit offers
  * Create listings

* Pending / Rejected / Expired:

  * Can view
  * Cannot participate

Display:

* Clear color indicator:

  * Green = Approved
  * Yellow = Pending
  * Red = Rejected
  * Orange = Expired

Important:

* Approval is manual (handled by us)
* No automation required
