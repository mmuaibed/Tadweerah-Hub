Before implementing:

## 1) Deal Value Summary (High Priority)

In the deal panel (or listing detail after offers):

Display:

* number of offers
* highest offer vs first offer
* difference (value gained)

Example:
"تم استلام 3 عروض. ارتفع السعر من 800 إلى 1050 ريال. القيمة الإضافية: 250 ريال"

This must be computed from existing offers — no external data.

---

## 2) Governance Timeline

In the deal panel:

Add a simple timeline:

* offer accepted
* payment confirmed
* dispatched
* received

Each step shows:

* label
* timestamp

No new states — just display existing ones clearly.

## 3) Printable Deal Report

Add a button:
"طباعة / تحميل تقرير الصفقة"

* unit + total price

"تمت عبر منصة تدويرة"
