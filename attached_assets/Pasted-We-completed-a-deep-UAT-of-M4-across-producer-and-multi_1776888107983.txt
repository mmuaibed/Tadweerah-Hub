We completed a deep UAT of M4 across producer and multiple buyer accounts.

Conclusion:
The core bidding / offer engine is working technically, but we found several important product, UX, operational, and governance gaps that should be addressed before moving forward.

Please review the following points one by one.

For each point, I want:
1. whether you agree or disagree
2. your reasoning
3. whether the proposed solution is the best MVP solution
4. if you have a better alternative, explain it clearly

Also:
After reviewing these points, please perform your own critical review of M4 and tell us if there are any additional important gaps we may have missed.

Do NOT implement yet.
First, give us your structured response and proposed M4.5 adjustment plan.

-----------------------------------
1) Pricing model
Issue:
The system currently uses price per unit, but real users think in total deal value.
This is creating confusion and unnecessary complexity.

Impact:
High — this affects usability, trust, and conversion.

Proposed solution:
Change buyer offer input from “price per unit” to “total offer value (SAR)”.
If needed, the system may calculate unit price internally, but the user-facing model should be total offer value.

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
2) Highest-offer visibility and buyer status clarity
Issue:
Buyers do not clearly understand:
- what the current highest offer is
- whether they are the highest bidder
- whether they have been outbid

Impact:
High — weakens the competitive loop and causes decision confusion.

Proposed solution:
In listing detail, clearly show:
- current highest offer
- buyer status:
  a) “You are currently the highest bidder”
  b) “You have been outbid”
For marketplace cards, also show lightweight competition signals.

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
3) Marketplace card signals
Issue:
The marketplace card does not make the bidding state visible enough.

Impact:
Medium to high — reduces engagement and urgency.

Proposed solution:
For open listings, show on the card:
- highest current offer
- number of offers
For closed listings, show:
- closed / sold state
- final accepted offer if appropriate, or at least clear closure status

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
4) No buyer-facing history / participation page
Issue:
After a listing is closed, buyers lose visibility and cannot easily see what happened to their offers.

Impact:
Critical — breaks the buyer journey after the most important moment.

Proposed solution:
Add a buyer-facing page such as:
- “My Offers”
or
- “My Participations”
This page should show:
- listings the buyer participated in
- current status:
  pending / accepted / rejected / closed

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
5) No post-decision experience after acceptance/rejection
Issue:
Winning and losing buyers do not get a clear end-state experience.
The listing disappears from the normal marketplace, but there is no clear result communication.

Impact:
Critical — this damages trust and operational clarity.

Proposed solution:
After producer decision:
- winning buyer sees a clear accepted state
- rejected buyers see a clear rejected state
- both should remain able to access the historical result from their buyer-side area

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
6) Rejection without reason
Issue:
The producer can reject an offer without giving a reason.

Impact:
High from a governance / fairness / operational standpoint.

Proposed solution:
Require a rejection reason when rejecting an offer manually.
At MVP level, even a short required text or selected reason is enough.

Please evaluate whether you agree.

-----------------------------------
7) Notifications
Issue:
Users are not informed when:
- a higher offer appears
- their offer is rejected
- their offer is accepted

Impact:
High — users must keep checking manually, which weakens engagement.

Proposed solution:
For MVP, we likely need a lightweight notification layer.
Possible directions:
- in-app status visibility first
- email notifications for key events
Please recommend the best MVP-safe notification strategy.

-----------------------------------
8) Competition timing / bid closing logic
Issue:
The current flow creates competitive bidding, but there is no visible timing logic or clear user expectation around when bidding ends.

Impact:
Medium to high — weakens urgency and may create ambiguity.

Proposed solution:
Please evaluate the best MVP approach:
Option A:
Keep producer-controlled acceptance without fixed countdown
Option B:
Introduce a visible deadline / closing time per listing
We want your recommendation on which is better for MVP and why.

-----------------------------------
9) Acceptance of lower offer than the highest one
Issue:
The producer may reject the highest offer and accept a lower one.
This may be commercially valid, but it creates fairness and governance questions.

Impact:
High from a trust and governance perspective.

Proposed solution:
If a lower offer is accepted while a higher offer exists or existed, require a reason from the producer.
Please evaluate whether this should be part of MVP.
