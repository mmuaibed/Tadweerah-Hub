# Antigravity External AI Review: UI/UX Surface Report

**Date:** 2026-07-03

### Summary of Findings
The visual surface of the Tadweerah platform presents a mature, modern B2B SaaS experience with strong trust signals and professional component design. The homepage, authentication flows, and general marketplace layouts are visually ready for partner and regulator demonstrations. However, critical UX gaps exist where functional logic or backend data structures undermine the interface. Most notably, the transport-responsibility indicator actively contradicts earlier user selections during the deal lifecycle, which could severely damage trust if demoed live. Additionally, the payment-proof submission form presents a severe dead-end due to conflicting "optional" labeling and required enforcement, while bilingual presentation is compromised by a schema limitation that forces single-language free text into cross-language contexts. Overall, the visual design is solid, but specific functional and data-driven UX friction points require resolution before comprehensive external exposure.
